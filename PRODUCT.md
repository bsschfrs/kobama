# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static-first; no framework runtime overhead for a marketing site, room for
components/partials if the site grows more pages later).

## Users

Owners and decision-makers at small-to-medium businesses who are considering a
website upgrade. They have long, careful decision timelines (this is not an
impulse buy) and are weighing KOBAMA against either a traditional agency or a
DIY builder (Wix/Squarespace). They want a premium, custom result without
having to manage the process themselves.

## Product Purpose

KOBAMA builds websites for small-to-medium businesses as a fully managed
service: strategy, design, development, and launch, end to end. The client
supplies input and approval, not labor. This site is KOBAMA's own marketing
and services site — its job is to build trust with exactly the audience above
and drive one action: requesting a conversation.

## Positioning

A fully managed website upgrade for SMBs — premium custom design without the
agency hassle. Distinct from a traditional agency (slower, pricier, more
process for the client to manage) and from DIY builders like Wix/Squarespace
(cheap but the client does the design/build work themselves). KOBAMA's
mechanism is: you get agency-grade craft, but the workload sits with KOBAMA,
not you.

## Operating Context

- Dutch-language copy (see DESIGN-BRIEF.md).
- Amsterdam-based.
- Single conversion path: one contact CTA ("plan een gesprek" / request a
  conversation). No self-serve signup, no in-product trial.
- B2B buyers with long consideration cycles — the site's job is sustained
  credibility, not urgency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a services/company site, not a SaaS product landing page and not a
  webshop.
- No pricing tiers, no "get started free," no self-service signup flow.
- Single CTA funnel throughout (nav, hero, closing section all point to the
  same contact action).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: KOBAMA.
- Based in Amsterdam.
- Full visual world (color tokens, typography scale, the Amsterdam facade-line
  motif, section structure, motion rules, copy voice) is already specified in
  [DESIGN-BRIEF.md](DESIGN-BRIEF.md) — treat it as binding visual authority
  alongside reference images in `References/`. Two decisions inside it are
  still explicitly open: dusk color temperature (warm amber vs. cooler
  violet-pink) and whether real photography gets added later.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No real client projects, testimonials, case studies, or team bios
exist to reference. Future work must not fabricate clients, results,
testimonials, or team members — the References/cases and Mensen (people)
sections should be built with real structure and placeholder markers
(clearly labeled as such) rather than invented content, until real content is
supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Every section serves the single contact CTA — no parallel funnels, no
   pricing, no self-serve path.
2. Trust is earned through credibility signals (clarity of process, evident
   craft, real references once available), not conversion pressure tactics —
   long B2B decision cycles punish urgency tricks.
3. Copy and structure reflect "we handle everything," not "here are tools to
   do it yourself" — reinforce the full-service mechanism at every
   opportunity.
4. The design's own craft is part of the pitch (see DESIGN-BRIEF's explicit
   anti-AI-slop stance) — execution quality has to be real, not asserted.
5. No fabricated evidence, ever — placeholders are honest; invented
   testimonials or client names are not.
